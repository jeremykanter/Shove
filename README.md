# Shove

Shove is a keyboard-based window manager for macOS. The way Shove works is simple, but provides a lot of flexibility:

* Press a keyboard shortcut to send a window to one side of the screen or the other.
* Keep pressing that shortcut to squeeze it into a smaller width, or press the shortcut for the opposite direction while continuing to hold down the modifier keys to expand it.
* Other shortcuts let you center windows, reduce their height, or move them to the top corners of the screen.
* Window sizes snap to a 12x4 grid, offering consistent sizes and a nice balance of speed and granularity.

Shove is inspired by the window management solutions offered by [Moom](https://manytricks.com/moom/) and [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com). If Shove isn't for you, I would encourage you to check out those great apps.

Shove is free and open source. If you find it useful, encounter bugs, or have ideas for how I can make it better, I’d love to hear from you!
